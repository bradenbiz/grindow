## Generation prompt

A premium macOS rounded-square icon body centered on a genuinely transparent canvas, with standard generous macOS icon padding (body approximately 824 by 824, centered), soft restrained shadow. Rich system-accent blue body with a subtle luminous gradient, precise softly beveled edges, refined depth, no excessive glass reflections. Centered on the blue body is a bold, beautifully balanced two-row by three-column grid of six rounded rectangular desktop tiles. Five tiles are soft translucent pale blue, the bottom middle tile is bright opaque pearl white and slightly raised, clearly marking the active Space. Strong clean silhouette that reads at 32px. The desktop rectangles should be simple solid panes with no tiny window controls or fine lines. Straight-on view, almost orthographic, highly refined macOS utility aesthetic, tactile but restrained. No text, no letters, no additional symbols, no arrows, no surrounding presentation, no mockup, no watermarks. Actual transparent alpha outside the rounded-square icon, not a checkerboard drawing. Deliver only the icon.
